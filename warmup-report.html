Section 02

The Warmup Strategy

ISPs don't know new sending domains. They watch for signals: who opens, who clicks, who marks as spam. The warmup ramp was designed to prove trust before scaling — and to adapt when strategy shifted.

After the first Vellum warmup sends, Fardad wanted to pivot toward a giveaway — less product pressure, more audience engagement while managing stock. The strategy adapted in real-time, weaving giveaway mechanics into the warmup sequence.

The Plan: Start small with highest-trust customers. Build engagement signal. Scale up as ISPs confirm deliverability. Complete warmup in under 2 weeks.
