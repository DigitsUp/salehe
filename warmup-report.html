01
The Foundation

Before We Sent a Single Email, We Cleaned Everything.

You had nearly a million email addresses — 5 to 8 years of history. Most businesses would blast the whole list and hope for the best. That's how you end up in spam.

Instead, we went through the entire list before sending a single message. We used a tool called Kickbox to verify every address — removing the ones that were dead, risky, or likely to bounce. Then we made a critical choice: start only with the people who already bought from you.

02
The Warmup

We Sent Small. We Scaled Up. We Adapted.

Email providers like Gmail and Outlook are watching. When they see a brand suddenly send to hundreds of thousands of people at once, they get suspicious — and your emails land in spam.

The warmup is the solution. You start small and earn trust slowly. Every day, we sent to a slightly bigger group. Real people opened. Real people clicked. The inbox providers watched, saw genuine engagement, and started trusting the email address. By the end of 12 days, our emails reliably land in the inbox — not spam.

Partway through, we also adapted the strategy in real time. After the first Vellum product sends, we shifted to a giveaway campaign — less product pressure, more pure engagement. This kept momentum strong while stock was being managed. About 6,000 new people signed up because of it.
